INVESTMENT TRACKER - COMPLETE FEATURE LIST

MOBILE COMPANION - INITIAL RELEASE
---------------------------------
- Android Capacitor project sharing the existing login, screens and backend
- Personal income/expense/refund/transfer/bill ledger with integer paise amounts
- Monthly confirmed totals, review/edit/reject inbox, web CSV, JSON backup/restore
- Ledger account/card and member links, member/account filters and per-member totals
- Device-local SMS member preference; unique account/owner matching with review
- Possible duplicate warnings for shared accounts; no automatic rejection
- Local bank/card SMS parsing and opt-in, user-triggered Android inbox scanning
- Initial templates: HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI, OneCard and YES BANK
- SMS imports remain pending until reviewed; identical rescans/retries deduplicated
- OTP/promo exclusion; no raw SMS storage/upload; last four account digits only
- Not yet: background SMS listener, automatic card reconciliation,
  push reminders, offline sync, biometric lock or native secure token storage
- Ledger totals are separate from investment cashflow and credit-card balances
- Full mobile roadmap: MOBILE_FEATURES.md; build guide: frontend/ANDROID.md

HOUSEHOLD FINANCE & PLANNING
---------------------------
- Bank, cash and wallet accounts with dated balance history and stale warnings
- Recorded balances only; no live bank connection or automatic ledger reconciliation
- Monthly confirmed income/expenses, savings rate and top spending categories
- Category budgets: warning at 80%, limit alert at 100% or more
- Dashboard money cards: liquid balance, monthly income/expense, budgets and cover
- Insurance policies, premium schedules, cover gaps and estimated 80C/80D premiums
- Gold and property holdings included in net worth
- Family members with occupation, date of birth, residency, disability, spouse link and declared tax regime
- Joint ownership splits (%) and "funded by" for every asset/liability; per-member assets, liabilities and net worth
- Monthly payslips per member and FY (fixed + variable pay, EPF, employer NPS, PT, TDS, rent) with projection of remaining months; pension, business (44AD/44ADA/actual) and other income
- FIRE planner: target corpus, required savings, retirement age and simulation
- Document vault: 10 MB per file; files excluded from JSON backups
- Zerodha/Groww CSV preview and import, with duplicate-trade detection
- Optional SMTP email/Telegram reminders configured per user in Settings
- SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_USERNAME, SMTP_PASSWORD, SMTP_FROM, TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
- Authenticated JWT refresh endpoint for companion clients
- Backup/restore and CSV export include the new financial records

- Reset Password (token-based)
- Change Password (authenticated)
- Profile / Account management
- Admin seed account (auto-created on startup)
- Admin: List all users

2. DASHBOARD & PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW
----------------------------------
- Portfolio summary (total invested, current value, total gains, overall return %)
- Portfolio XIRR (time-weighted return across all investments)
- Asset allocation pie chart (by investment category)
- Per-category summaries (MF, Stocks, Bonds, FD, PF, NPS — invested/current/XIRR)
- Loan summaries (home loan + personal loan outstanding, EMI, prepayments)
- Credit card summary (outstanding, total limit, avg utilization)
- Risk metrics (equity/debt ratio, risk score, diversification, loan-to-asset ratio, concentration risk)
- Upcoming payouts (next 30 days — bond interest, FD maturity, principal repayments)
- ELSS tax saving (Section 80C ELSS total in current FY)
- Rebalancing suggestions (user provides target allocation, system suggests buy/sell)

3. INVESTMENT MODULES
---------------------

3a. Mutual Funds
  - CRUD for fund holdings
  - Transaction tracking (purchase, SIP, redemption, switch-in, switch-out)
  - CAS PDF import (Consolidated Account Statement bulk import)
  - Auto NAV fetch from AMFI API (by scheme code)
  - Bulk NAV refresh for all funds
  - Recalculate all fund totals
  - ELSS 3-year lock-in tracking per transaction

3b. Stocks
  - CRUD for stock holdings
  - Buy/sell transaction tracking
  - Dividend recording
  - Live price fetch via Yahoo Finance API
  - Auto-refresh every 60 seconds during market hours (Mon-Fri 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST)
  - Market status check (Indian stock market open/closed)
  - Average cost calculation (running avg cost method)

3c. Corporate Bonds
  - CRUD for bond holdings
  - Auto-generated interest payout schedules (monthly/quarterly/biannually/annually)
  - Principal repayment schedules (bullet or staggered maturity)
  - Mark interest/principal payouts as received
  - Dynamic recalculation when principal reduces (staggered bonds)

3d. Fixed Deposits
  - CRUD for FD records
  - Maturity amount calculation (cumulative compound / non-cumulative simple interest)
  - Auto-renewal tracking

3e. Provident Fund (EPF / VPF / PPF)
  - CRUD for PF accounts
  - Monthly contribution tracking by financial year (employee + employer split)
  - PF passbook PDF import (bulk import with OCR)
  - Interest calculation (monthly running balance method per EPFO rules)

3f. Home Loans
  - CRUD for home loans
  - EMI payment recording
  - Lump-sum prepayment tracking
  - Interest rate change recording (floating rate loans)
  - Full amortization schedule generation
  - Under-construction support (tranche disbursements, pre-EMI interest, mark construction complete)
  - Recalculate after changes

3g. Personal Loans
  - CRUD for personal loans
  - EMI payment recording
  - Prepayment tracking
  - Interest rate change recording
  - Full amortization schedule generation

3h. NPS (National Pension System)
  - CRUD for NPS accounts (Tier 1 / Tier 2)
  - Contribution tracking with date
  - Asset allocation tracking (equity, corporate bond, govt securities, alternate assets)
  - Fund manager tracking (SBI, LIC, HDFC, etc.)

3h-2. Vehicle Rentals (EV fleet leasing, e.g. Ridoji)
  - Track vehicles bought and leased to a fleet operator for a fixed monthly rental
  - Auto-generated monthly payout schedule (units x payout per unit, for the agreement tenure)
  - Payouts are confirmed manually (date + actual amount); unpaid payouts >5 days late are flagged overdue
  - "Mark all due as received", undo, short-payment tracking, buyback tracking
  - Straight-line book value (purchase cost -> buyback value), capital recovered %, net return, XIRR
  - Overdue payout and agreement-end alerts; included in dashboard, net worth, cashflow, backup and CSV export

3i. Credit Cards
  - CRUD for credit cards
  - Monthly billing statement recording
  - Statement payment tracking
  - Individual card transaction recording
  - Card EMI conversion tracking
  - Credit score history recording

3j. SIPs (Systematic Investment Plans)
  - CRUD for SIP plans
  - Installment payment tracking
  - Link SIPs to mutual fund holdings
  - Frequency and SIP date tracking

4. ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENCE
----------------------------
- Investment Projections (Monte Carlo — 2000 jointly simulated, market-correlated paths; P10/P50/P90 bands; 1-30 year horizon; XIRR blended with category long-term returns; scheduled cashflows for FD/PF/bonds/vehicle rentals; bull/bear/base scenarios on market-linked returns; inflation-adjusted view; net-worth projection with loan amortisation)
- Cashflow Analysis (monthly inflow/outflow aggregation across all investments, month drilldown)
- Insights Engine (portfolio analysis — risk, performance, tax, diversification, loan, goal, and opportunity insights with severity levels: critical/warning/info/positive)
- Benchmark Comparison (Nifty 50 and Sensex via Yahoo Finance: 1Y/3Y/5Y returns with CAGR, hourly cache, 1m-5y chart periods)
- Tax Center (household or per-member Section 80C: EPF + PPF + ELSS + home loan principal + life insurance; 80D self/parents; 24b: home loan interest; 80CCD: NPS Tier-1; capital gains with debt-fund gains at slab)
- Family tax: per-member old vs new regime for FY 2025-26 / 2026-27 (Income-tax Act, 2025 section numbers), clubbing rules (switchable for spouse / minor income), 87A rebate + marginal relief, surcharge, cess, senior slabs, break-even deductions, household saving suggestions
- "Where to invest" advisor: ranks members by post-tax return for FD, bonds, debt/equity funds, ELSS, PPF, SSY, SCSS, NPS
- Net Worth Tracking (real-time assets minus liabilities, daily snapshots, historical trends)
- Financial Goals (target amount/date with optional inflation, link investments to goals (≤100% per investment across goals), on-track based on actual SIP/PF/NPS contributions, auto return from linked investments, shortfall and extra monthly investment needed, batch linking)
